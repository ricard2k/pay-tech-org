# Compliance & Standards

This final module covers the regulatory landscape, industry standards, and compliance requirements for payment systems.

## Key Standards

- **PCI-DSS** (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)
- **EMV** (Europay, Mastercard, Visa) specifications
- **ISO Standards** for payment systems
- **Regional Requirements** (GDPR, local banking regulations)

## Topics Covered

- **PCI-DSS Requirements** - Security standards and certification
- **EMV Compliance** - Chip card requirements and certification
- **Data Privacy** - GDPR and regional data protection laws
- **Audit & Reporting** - Compliance audits and incident reporting
- **Certification Processes** - How to certify payment systems

## Compliance Checklist

This module will help you understand:
- ✓ What compliance certifications are needed
- ✓ How to implement compliant systems
- ✓ Common compliance failures and how to avoid them
- ✓ Documentation and audit requirements
- ✓ Incident response and reporting
