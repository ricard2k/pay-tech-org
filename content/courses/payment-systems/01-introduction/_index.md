# Introduction to Payment Systems

## What Are Payment Systems?

Payment systems are the infrastructure and protocols that enable the transfer of money between parties. They involve:

- **Hardware**: Point of Sale (POS) terminals, payment processors, card readers
- **Software**: Transaction processing, encryption, authentication systems
- **Networks**: Banks, payment gateways, card networks (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
- **Standards**: EMV, PCI-DSS, and security protocols

## Why Cryptography Matters

Modern payment systems rely heavily on cryptography to:

- **Authenticate**: Verify that transactions are legitimate
- **Encrypt**: Protect sensitive card data in transit and at rest
- **Authorize**: Ensure only authorized parties can approve transactions
- **Audit**: Create tamper-proof transaction logs
